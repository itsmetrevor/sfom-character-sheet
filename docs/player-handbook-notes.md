## Cover (p001)

SHARD — Fragments of Memory — Player Handbook. A multi-class, dynamic-combat roleplaying tabletop system. Cover art: a star-faceted blue crystal shard ringed by adventurer silhouettes (crown, staff, bow, flasks) over a background of faint skill icons.

## Character Sheet (p002–p003)

Left side (Roleplay): Character Info (name plus Ancestry/Background details). Levels (Class Levels C.LVL, Party Level P.LVL). Expertise (18 total, each tied to a Main Stat; raised via classes and Party Levels). Non-Combat Passive Abilities (Ancestry passive plus any unlocked class passive; another slot at Party Level 4). Background Passive Abilities (unlock at Party Levels 0, 3, 6, 9). Class Levels (track progress toward unlocking new classes).

Right side (Combat): HP / Energy / Status Effects (MAX HP, damage taken, energy spent, conditions). Character Stats (Main Stats for checks and damage; Bonus Stats for mobility, protection, luck, Energy). Combat Passive Abilities (Ancestry passive plus two unlocked class passives; another slot at Party Level 8). Equipment (Main Hand, Armor, Off Hand per Main Class; Accessories free). Inventory (stacks of 5; usable in and out of combat). Shards / Gold (Shards from Party Levels and the GM; gold tracked alongside).

## Character Creation (p004–p005)

Work with the GM on a backstory, then follow the next sections (Ancestries, Backgrounds, Classes).

Stat shapes: square = permanent (never changes). Circle = temporary (gear, passives, effects; changes). 4-pointed star = class stats (follow your current class). Small diamond pip = permanent +1 Main stat from Party Level rewards (max twice per Main stat).

Party Level stat upgrade: fill diamond pips for permanent Main Stat rewards, at most twice each.

Step 1 — Base stats (right side squares): Max HP 30. Main Stats: STR 1, DEX 1, CON 1, MND 1, INT 1, LUK 1. Bonus Stats: JMP 1, AGL 5, DEF 10, RES 10, NRG 1, FOR 1. (Class stats overlay these per Main Class.)

Step 2 — Ancestry. Left side: name, languages, vision; Ancestry Expertise as permanent increases; Non-Combat Ancestry Passive in the first active slot. Right side: permanent Max HP, Main Stats (+1 positive, -1 negative), Bonus Stat; Combat Ancestry Passive in the first active slot.

Step 3 — Background. Left side: Background Expertise (permanent) plus tier-unlocked Background passives. Right side: Background equipment/items into inventory.

Step 4 — Starting class (your Main Class): Chemist (Healer), Gladiator (Tank), Ranger (Ranged DPS), Scholar (Specialist), Soldier (Melee DPS), Trickster (Specialist). Copy each Class Stat star to the Character Sheet; write X = 1 at the top.

Step 5 — Starting Gear bundle of choice (see Gear section, page XX).

## Selecting an Ancestry (walkthrough, p005)

Read each Ancestry's history, culture, and religion, then record (left): name, languages, vision; Expertise into the square field; Non-Combat Passive into the top slot. Then (right): Max HP into the square; the 2 Main Stats (always 1 positive, 1 negative); the Bonus Stat; Combat Passive into the top slot.

Hybrid Ancestries (GM permitting): pick up to 2, but only 1 choice per bonus — 1 language (besides Common), 1 vision, 1 Ancestry's Expertise, 1 positive Main Stat, 1 negative Main Stat, 1 Bonus Stat, 1 Non-Combat and 1 Combat Passive — and always the lower Max HP of the two.

After selecting the Ancestry for your character you will then select a Background. Selecting a background helps determine what your character was like before starting your new Adventure. Each background is completely unique with various abilities that will come in handy to you and your Party. Once a Background is selected, gain the following:

- Gain 2 permanent points in specific Expertise.
- Add the 'Starting Items' from your Background to your Inventory.
- Each Background unlocks one ability per tier at Party Level 0, 3, 6, and 9, recorded on your 'Character Sheet' background-ability bar (ACTIVE tab; slots P.LVL 0/3/6/9; callouts Ability Name / Background Title / Ability Type / Party Level Unlock / Ability Description).

You may select up to 2 Backgrounds: gain Permanent Expertise from only 1; gain Starting Items from only 1; at each tier (Party Level 0, 3, 6, 9) choose 1 Ability from 1 Background (e.g. Barbarian & Psychologist: one Level 0 pick, one Level 3 pick, and so on).

## Craftsman backgrounds (shared rules) <!-- p031 -->

Four Craftsman backgrounds — Armorer, Blacksmith, Clothier, Jeweler — share crafting mechanics; each requires adventure resources, and resources impart effects on crafted Gear. See the Crafting Section, page XX.

Craftsman's Writ: each comes with a specific 'Writ' shown to shop/forge proprietors for facility access.

Efficiency: fewer items per craft by quality — Common 1 less, Uncommon 2 less, Rare 3 less.

Capabilities: Blacksmith — Weapons. Armorer — Medium & Heavy Armor. Clothier — Light Armor & Clothing Accessories. Jeweler — Jewelry Accessories.

- Party Level 9 — Craft Rare: You may Craft Rare Potions. Check the Alchemy Crafting Guide for more info. Detail: go to the ‘Alchemy Crafting Guide’ on page XX. When crafting potions you will be required to spend Materials from your Inventory. Each Potion will require specific types of Materials. ## What a Class is

As a Fragment you are capable of accessing and harnessing the power of memories that lay dormant inside the 'Source'. Unlike the common man, this power gives you the ability to utilize specialties from across multiple universes. The more you use this connection to the Source the stronger your tether to it becomes, allowing you to gain access to even more memories. Being a Fragment you can swap between these specialties and retain the knowledge you learn from one specialty and use it to enhance another.

At the start of your Adventure you will choose 1 Starter Class: Chemist, Gladiator, Ranger, Scholar, Soldier, or Trickster. Add the MAX HP and Stats on your 'Main Class' Sheet to your 'Character Sheet'.

Once you reach Class Level 10 with one of the Starter Classes you will gain access to the next tier of Classes. When swapping to a new Main Class, any previous Class can be used as a 'Sub Class': you may use any Abilities on the Right side of the Sub Class sheet but lose the left side. Overlay sheets to track this. Change your star-stats to the current Main Class, and you may write any unlocked passives from previous classes into Character Sheet passive slots.

## How to read a class sheet (p049 guide)

Left side: Class Info (icon, name, prerequisites, unlocks, role icons, X box — X modifies damage/effects, grows with Party Level, starts at 1). Stats (add class MAX HP/stats to Character Sheet). Ultimate Ability (combat-only; fill all 10 slots via listed actions, usable any time in combat without interrupting; counts as a Reaction). Combat Modifiers (how the class functions — read thoroughly). Stars (each Right-side unlock fills 1 Star on EACH Expertise and Locked Passives section; completed sections grant bonus rewards). Expertise (permanent increase when stars complete). Equipment (Main Hand / Armor / Off Hand — strict; unequipped slots count as bonus-less base gear).

Right side: Passives (Non-Combat + Combat passives start unlocked; usable freely as Main Class; learning all abilities grants another Combat Passive + a permanent stat). Class Level (20 max; GM awards fill locked-ability slots). Main Actions (1/round). Support Actions (1/round, or 2 if you skip your Move Action). Reactions (condition-triggered, usable off-turn).

## Chemist (Starter, INT, Physical Ranged Healer) <!-- p051 -->

Chemists aren't as advanced as an Alchemist but they can use Chemistry to craft potions from found ingredients. Their resourcefulness and quick crafting make them a great addition to a Party as a field medic. With their crafting abilities and quick hands they are capable of creating potions and tossing them across the battlefield with precision to those in need of healing.

Combat goal: keep the party alive by throwing potions; poison bombs and a Piercing (Ranged) weapon add DPS. Unlike magic healers, the Chemist uses physical Items — enabling 'Chain' into Restorative abilities, with fixed-HP restoration.

Combat Modifier — Enhanced Chemistry. Non-Combat Passive 'Chemistry': gather ingredients and craft Items from found ingredients; as Main Class, Enhanced Chemistry finds more ingredients. Crafted Items sit in inventory until used. 'Throwing' abilities spend inventory Items. Basic Alchemy: with any Throwing ability, roll D6 — 1-3 nothing, 4-5 +INT HP restored or +INT damage by item, 6 = item not spent.

Expertise: Crafting Expertise (efficient crafting; permanent increase after 2 abilities). Throw/Catch Expertise (thrown precision; permanent increase after 4 abilities).

Equipment: Main Hand Ranged (Piercing) — Pistol, no bonus. Armor Medium — basic, no bonus. Off Hand Dagger — melee, basic, no bonus.

Passives: Chemistry (per Long Rest, gather ingredients outside combat and craft). Throw Proficiency (throw inventory Items at Range 10 instead of 1). Mixologist (master Chemist: Items gain +INT damage and HP restored when spent).

Prerequisites: N/A. Unlocks: all Tier 1 [10] + Alchemist [15].
